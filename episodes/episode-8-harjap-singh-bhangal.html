Episode 8

Harjap Singh Bhangal on the realities of UK immigration law

29 May 2026 · Hosted by DJ Haashim · Filmed in London

In episode 8 we sit down with Harjap Singh Bhangal LLB, a British Punjabi solicitor of England and Wales specialising in immigration and nationality law.

Harjap has built a respected reputation in the legal field and, as the channel notes, was the first UK immigration solicitor to be featured in a BBC World Service documentary showing a day in the life of his work and the realities of immigration law in the UK.

The conversation covers the realities of UK immigration law as he sees it day to day: high pressure cases and client stories, the challenges facing migrant families, and what representation looks like within the British Asian community. Harjap also talks about building a successful legal career and the personal sacrifices that come with the profession.

It is an insightful hour, full of real experience and perspective from one of the UK's most recognised immigration solicitors.
