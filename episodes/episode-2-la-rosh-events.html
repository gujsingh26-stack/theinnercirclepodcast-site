Episode 2

La Rosh Events on luxury weddings and building a destination events brand

10 April 2026 · Hosted by DJ Haashim · Filmed in London

Episode 2 steps inside the world of luxury event planning with La Rosh Events, internationally recognised wedding and event planners known for creating timeless, high end experiences across Europe, the UAE and India.

We explore the journey behind building a luxury brand, what it really takes to execute a destination wedding, and the mindset required to stand out in the events industry. Expect real insight, behind the scenes stories and useful lessons for anyone trying to raise the level of their own business or creative work.

If you are passionate about entrepreneurship, luxury experiences or the events industry, this is an episode worth your time.

In this episode

  • Building a luxury events brand
  • What it takes to execute destination weddings
  • Working across Europe, the UAE and India
  • The mindset needed to stand out in the events industry

About La Rosh Events

La Rosh Events are wedding and event planners creating high end experiences across Europe, the UAE and India.
