Episode 12

Vidhi Rose on ambition, confidence and building the life you want

9 September 2026 · Hosted by DJ Haashim · Filmed in London

In episode 12, DJ Haashim sits down with Vidhi Rose for an open and honest conversation about her journey so far, the experiences that have shaped her, and what it really takes to build the life you want.

Vidhi shares her perspective on ambition, personal growth and navigating challenges while staying true to yourself as the goals get bigger. The conversation goes beyond the surface, into the mindset behind success, the lessons that only come from difficult moments, and the confidence it takes to follow your own path when the people around you expect something different.

Expect stories, reflection and plenty of moments that will make you think about your own next move.

In this episode

  • Vidhi Rose's journey so far
  • The experiences that shaped her
  • Ambition, confidence and personal growth
  • Overcoming challenges and setbacks
  • Staying authentic in a world of expectations
  • Building the right mindset for success
  • Lessons learned along the way
  • What the future holds for Vidhi
