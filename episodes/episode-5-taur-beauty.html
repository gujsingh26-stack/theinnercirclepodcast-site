Episode 5

Taur Beauty on the real world of influencing

30 April 2026 · Hosted by DJ Haashim · Filmed in London

Episode 5 steps inside the real world of influencing. We welcome Taur Beauty to talk about influencer life: the highs, the pressure, the behind the scenes reality, and what it really takes to build a brand online.

From the struggles of content creation to staying authentic in a curated world, this is an honest conversation about a career that looks effortless from the outside and rarely is.

Whether you are an aspiring creator or simply curious about influencer culture, this episode gives you the unfiltered version.

In this episode

  • The highs and the pressure of influencer life
  • The behind the scenes reality of content creation
  • What it really takes to build a brand online
  • Staying authentic in a curated world

About Taur Beauty

Taur Beauty is a beauty content creator and influencer.
