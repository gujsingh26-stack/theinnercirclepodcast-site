Episode 11

Bhavesh Dhamecha on building businesses in medical aesthetics

13 July 2026 · Hosted by DJ Haashim · Filmed in London

In episode 11 we sit down with Bhavesh Dhamecha, an entrepreneur and leader in the medical aesthetics and technology industry. Bhavesh is connected with Aesthethically, Lifinity Group and Biotec Italia UK, where he supports the growth of clinics through advanced aesthetic technologies, education and business strategy.

This conversation explores Bhavesh's entrepreneurial journey, the realities of building businesses, how the aesthetics industry has evolved, and the mindset required to keep moving forward through challenges.

We talk about building and scaling companies, developing an entrepreneurial mindset, the growth of medical aesthetics and the technology behind it, and how Bhavesh helps clinics grow and stay competitive. He is open about the lessons, risks and sacrifices behind success, and about his ambitions for what comes next.

Whether you are an entrepreneur, a clinic owner, an aesthetics professional or someone working towards your next big goal, this episode offers honest insight from someone with deep industry experience.

In this episode

  • Building and scaling businesses
  • Developing an entrepreneurial mindset
  • The growth of medical aesthetics and technology
  • Helping clinics grow and remain competitive
  • Innovation within the beauty and wellness industries
  • The lessons, risks and sacrifices behind success
  • Bhavesh's ambitions for the future
