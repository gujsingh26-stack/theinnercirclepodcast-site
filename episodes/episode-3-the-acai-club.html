Episode 3

The Açai Club on building a health brand with a real community

16 April 2026 · Hosted by DJ Haashim · Filmed in London

In episode 3 we sit down with the team behind The Açai Club to hear the story of one of the fastest growing health focused brands around. From humble beginnings to a strong community, the conversation is full of insight on entrepreneurship, branding and staying authentic in a crowded market.

We talk about how The Açai Club got started, the challenges of scaling a lifestyle brand, what it really takes to stand out in the wellness space, and how they have built a loyal community and customer base.

Whether you are an aspiring entrepreneur, a fitness enthusiast, or just someone who loves a good açai bowl, there is real value in this one.

In this episode

  • How The Açai Club got started
  • The challenges of scaling a lifestyle brand
  • What it really takes to stand out in the wellness space
  • Building a loyal community and customer base

About The Açai Club

The Açai Club is a health focused food and lifestyle brand.
