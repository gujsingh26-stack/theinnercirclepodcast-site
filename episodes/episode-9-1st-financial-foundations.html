Episode 9

1st Financial Foundations on building wealth and protecting your future

8 June 2026 · Hosted by DJ Haashim · Filmed in London

In episode 9, Haashim sits down with the team from 1st Financial Foundations to talk about money, financial planning and the importance of building a secure future.

Founded in 2001, 1st Financial Foundations is a team of independent financial advisers helping professionals, business owners, self employed people and families gain greater financial confidence and freedom. Their work covers mortgages, pensions, investments, protection, wealth planning and inheritance tax planning.

The conversation covers why financial planning matters at every stage of life, the common money mistakes people make, how mortgages, pensions and long term investments fit together, and how to protect yourself, your family and your business. The team also share the story behind growing the firm and why they believe financial education can change someone's future.

It is a practical episode for anyone who wants to take more control of their finances and make better long term decisions.

Please note: this episode is for general information only and does not constitute personal financial advice. Always speak to a qualified financial adviser before making financial decisions.

In this episode

  • Why financial planning is important at every stage of life
  • Common money mistakes people make
  • Mortgages, pensions and long term investments
  • Protecting yourself, your family and your business
  • Building wealth with a clear and realistic strategy
  • The journey behind growing 1st Financial Foundations
  • How financial education can change your future
