Episode 7

Eishdeep Benning: selling her house to open an Anytime Fitness gym

15 May 2026 · Hosted by DJ Haashim · Filmed in London

In episode 7 we sit down with Eishdeep Benning, a South Asian entrepreneur with a story of sacrifice, belief and ambition. Eishdeep sold her house to pursue her dream of opening an Anytime Fitness gym, and she talks openly about what it really takes to build something from the ground up.

The conversation covers business and risk taking, the mindset needed to bet on yourself, and the role of family and culture in that decision. Eishdeep also speaks about the particular challenges of being a South Asian woman in entrepreneurship and how she has navigated them.

This is an episode for anyone chasing a dream, building a business, or needing a push to believe in themselves.

In this episode

  • Selling her house to open an Anytime Fitness gym
  • Business, risk taking and backing yourself
  • Mindset, family and culture
  • The challenges of being a South Asian woman in entrepreneurship
  • What it really takes to build something from the ground up

About Eishdeep Benning

Eishdeep Benning is an entrepreneur and Anytime Fitness gym owner.
