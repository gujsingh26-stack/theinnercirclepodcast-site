Episode 1

Pagh Vala on identity, culture and building a brand with purpose

2 April 2026 · Hosted by DJ Haashim · Filmed in London

The first ever episode of The Inner Circle Podcast. We sit down with the team behind Pagh Vala, a brand redefining identity, culture and modern storytelling.

We dive into the meaning and vision behind Pagh Vala, what it means to build a brand rooted in heritage and authenticity, and the challenges of standing out in a crowded space. There are lessons on entrepreneurship, creativity and resilience, and a look at what comes next for the movement.

This is not just a conversation about business. It is about purpose, representation and creating something that truly connects. Whether you are an entrepreneur, a creative or simply looking for inspiration, it is a strong place to start the series.

In this episode

  • The meaning and vision behind Pagh Vala
  • Building a brand rooted in heritage and authenticity
  • The challenges of standing out in today's crowded space
  • Lessons on entrepreneurship, creativity and resilience
  • What's next for the movement

About Pagh Vala

Pagh Vala is a brand built around identity, culture and modern storytelling.
