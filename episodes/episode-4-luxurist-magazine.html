Episode 4

The founders of Luxurist Magazine on building a luxury media brand

4 May 2026 · Hosted by DJ Haashim · Filmed in London

In episode 4 we are joined by Anisha Vasani and Zamiha Desai, the founders of Luxurist Magazine, a growing name in the luxury lifestyle and media space.

The conversation covers the vision behind Luxurist Magazine, what it takes to build a brand in a crowded market, and how business growth and media influence feed each other. Anisha and Zamiha are open about the challenges they have faced as founders and what success actually means to them in today's world.

If you are interested in business, branding and hearing from founders who are building something of their own, this one is for you.

In this episode

  • The vision behind Luxurist Magazine
  • Entrepreneurship and building a brand
  • Business growth and media influence
  • Overcoming challenges as founders
  • What success means in today's world

About Luxurist Magazine

Anisha Vasani and Zamiha Desai are the founders of Luxurist Magazine, a luxury lifestyle and media brand.
