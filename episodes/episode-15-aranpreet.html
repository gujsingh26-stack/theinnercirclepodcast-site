Episode 15

Aranpreet on beauty, social media and building By Aran

29 September 2026 · Hosted by DJ Haashim · Filmed in London

In episode 15, DJ Haashim sits down with Aranpreet, known online as Beauty With Aranpreet, for an open conversation about her journey through the beauty industry, building her presence on social media and stepping into a new chapter with her own venture, By Aran.

Aranpreet talks about what it has taken to build a personal brand from scratch: the consistency it demands, the pressures that come with living part of your life in public, and how she developed the confidence to call herself an entrepreneur rather than just a creator. She is candid about the gap between how a business looks on Instagram and what it takes behind the scenes to turn an idea into something real.

The conversation also gets into the reality of building something of your own. The lessons learned along the way, the moments of doubt, and what keeps Aranpreet motivated to keep growing and evolving when the easy option would be to stand still.

If you work in beauty, create content, or are thinking about turning a following into a business, this is an honest look at how one person is doing exactly that.

In this episode

  • Aranpreet's journey through the beauty industry
  • Building a personal brand on social media
  • Dealing with the pressure that comes with a public profile
  • Finding confidence as an entrepreneur
  • Turning an idea into a real business with By Aran
  • The lessons behind building something of your own
  • What keeps her motivated to keep growing

About Aranpreet

Aranpreet is a beauty creator and entrepreneur, known on Instagram as Beauty With Aranpreet, and the founder of By Aran.
