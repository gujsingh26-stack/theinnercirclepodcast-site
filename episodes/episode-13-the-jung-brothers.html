Episode 13

The Jung Brothers on South Asians in football, family and legacy

15 September 2026 · Hosted by DJ Haashim · Filmed in London

In episode 13, DJ Haashim sits down with brothers Arjun Jung and Karun Jung for a conversation about football, family, representation and creating opportunities for the next generation.

Arjun and Karun share their individual journeys through the game, the work being done through Sporttiers, and their passion for helping more young people, particularly those from South Asian backgrounds, believe there is a place for them in football.

The conversation gets into the realities of South Asian representation in the sport: why so few players reach the professional game, the barriers young players can face, the importance of coaching and grassroots development, and what parents can do to support children who want to pursue football seriously.

The brothers also open up about growing up together, the competition between them, family values, setbacks, discipline, and the legacy they want to leave behind. As they put it, this conversation is bigger than football. It is about representation, opportunity and showing the next generation what is possible.

In this episode

  • Arjun and Karun's football journeys
  • Growing up as the Jung brothers
  • South Asian representation in football
  • Why so few South Asians reach the professional game
  • Breaking stereotypes and cultural barriers
  • Grassroots football and youth development
  • Karun's work with Sporttiers
  • The role of parents in youth football
  • Talent versus discipline
  • Brotherhood, family and legacy

About The Jung Brothers

Arjun Jung and Karun Jung are brothers with backgrounds in football. Karun works with Sporttiers, supporting young players and grassroots development.
