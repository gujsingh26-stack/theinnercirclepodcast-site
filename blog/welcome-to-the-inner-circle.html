Welcome to The Inner Circle

1 October 2026 · DJ Haashim

The Inner Circle is a long form conversation series. One guest, one host, one hour, no rush. We sit down with founders, creators and innovators and talk properly about business, mindset and entertainment: how they started, what nearly broke them, and what they know now that they wish they had known at the beginning.

Who it is for

It is for the person building something on the side while holding down a job. The person who has just left a safe career to go it alone. The creator who is three videos in and wondering whether anyone is watching. If you are a young professional in the UK trying to work out your next move, these conversations are for you.

Our guests come from every direction: business owners, performers, writers, athletes, people in media and people in trades. What they have in common is that they did the thing, and they are honest about how it went.

Why we film it like a film

Most podcasts are recorded. This one is produced. We film in a studio in London with multiple cameras, proper lighting and a set built for the show, and every episode is edited by a production team before it goes out. The reason is simple. The people we sit down with have put years into their craft, and the conversation should look as considered as the work they are describing.

It also changes the conversation itself. When the room looks and feels like a set, guests sit up, slow down and say the thing they would not say on a video call. The best moments on this show have come from that.

One guest, one host, one hour, no rush.

Where to find it

Full episodes go out on YouTube, and you can listen on Spotify through our links page. The best moments are cut into clips for Instagram and TikTok, so you can catch up in a minute or sit down for the whole thing.
